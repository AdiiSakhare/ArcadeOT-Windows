# Analog I/O

Voltage/current, RTD and thermocouple interfaces are distinct. Record signal range, channel settings, module resolution/raw convention, fault diagnostics and isolation from exact module manuals. A 4–20 mA transmitter is not interchangeable with a voltage input; RTD/thermocouple channels require their approved wiring and compensation. No universal raw scaling is assumed.

See Model-Specific-Terminal-References.md for official manual pointers. Exact terminal numbers and wiring instructions are intentionally not asserted without an identified SKU/module.
