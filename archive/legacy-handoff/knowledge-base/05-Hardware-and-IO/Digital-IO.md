# Digital I/O

Read actual module voltage/input logic and source/sink compatibility before connecting a sensor. NPN/PNP, relay/transistor and common-group behavior depend on exact hardware. A PLC output-state bit may not prove physical contact or load state. Do not wire from brand-level examples. App reads mapped memory only; it does not configure electrical I/O.

See Model-Specific-Terminal-References.md for official manual pointers. Exact terminal numbers and wiring instructions are intentionally not asserted without an identified SKU/module.
